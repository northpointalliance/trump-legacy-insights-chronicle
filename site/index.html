How many U.S. bankruptcy filings occurred in 2025?

Total 2025 filings reached 565,759, up 11.2% vs 508,953 in 2024. Consumer cases drove most of the increase amid elevated rates, card debt above $1.2T, and post-pandemic normalization.

How has inbound U.S. travel changed since 2025?

International visitor volumes trended down year-over-year through late 2025 while global tourism spending elsewhere rose. Canada and UK arrivals showed double-digit declines in some months; Japan and Dominican Republic were exceptions with gains.

Firings, Turnover & Loyalty Tests

Cabinet churn, removal of officials who contradicted the president, and parallel exits in Congress, 2017–2021.

How unstable was first-term leadership?

The Brookings Institution tracked higher-than-normal Cabinet turnover. Trump removed or accepted resignations from officials who contradicted him on Russia, Ukraine, Syria, or election integrity, including FBI Director James Comey (2017), Secretary of State Rex Tillerson (2018), Defense Secretary James Mattis (2018), Attorney General Jeff Sessions (2018), and Homeland Security officials who resisted family-separation policy shifts.

By many counts, the White House had four chiefs of staff, four national security advisers, and multiple acting secretaries across departments.

What happened in Congress during the same period?

Roughly 190 House and Senate departures across the 115th and 116th Congresses included resignations for administration posts, scandals, and a wave of Republican retirements before the 2018 midterms.
